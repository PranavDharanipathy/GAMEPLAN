The match

Points

Your robot

A mecanum drive with a roller intake, a riser and a hooded flywheel launcher. The flywheel spins all match; its speed and the hood follow your distance to your HIVE's up-facing CELL. Hold Fire and the gate opens only for a ready shot (light green). With auto-aim on (Settings > Gameplay) the robot also turns to face the CELL while you keep driving.

A match recorded on a computer

Matches played with GamePlan on a computer, including ones driven by your team's own code, can be watched here. Export one with gp-sim replay-export out/<match> and choose the .gpreplay file, or drop it here.

Space plays and pauses, the arrow keys skip 5 s (with Shift: to the previous or next event), [ and ] change speed, C changes camera, 1–4 pick a robot, Esc leaves. Gamepad: A, LB/RB, d-pad, Y, B.
